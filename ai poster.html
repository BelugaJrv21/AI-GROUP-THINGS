THE OPERATOR DATA PLATE
// READ BEFORE EVERY SHIFT
Pre-Op Check
Walk the forklift before you sit down. Hoses, forks, mast, tyres, fluids — if it fails, it doesn't move.
Know the Limit
Never exceed the rated load capacity. An overloaded forklift is an unstable forklift — full stop.
8 km/h Indoors
Slow through aisles, doorways, and blind corners. Speed is the difference between a near-miss and an incident.
Pedestrian Zone
Make eye contact with pedestrians before moving. If you can't see them, assume they can't see you either.
Stability Triangle
Every forklift balances on an invisible triangle between its front wheels and rear axle. Raise a load, tilt on a slope, or corner too fast, and that triangle tips. Keep loads low while travelling and never turn on a ramp.
